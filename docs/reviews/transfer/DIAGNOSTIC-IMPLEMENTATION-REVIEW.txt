Two reliability items remain before consuming the unique target-state attempt:

   the fixed BusyBox ash interactive editing buffer. The current line is 1419

   next call is missed. Preserve a bounded scanner suffix/cursor in Probe
   across both calls; keep the overall count at two and record fixed replies.
   Add a test spanning the two calls, not just two chunks of one call.
